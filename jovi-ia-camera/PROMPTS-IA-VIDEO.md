# Prompts para gerar as cenas reais com IA (Veo 3, Kling, Runway, Sora ou Gemini)

**Formato de todos os vídeos: vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 ou 30 fps.**

Gere primeiro a **foto de referência (passo 0)** e use essa imagem como referência (image-to-video ou "character reference") em todos os outros prompts. Assim a mesma atriz aparece em todas as cenas.

Os prompts estão em inglês porque os geradores entendem melhor. As falas estão em português e devem continuar em português.

---

## Personagem (cole este bloco em todos os prompts)
> CHARACTER: Brazilian woman, 20 years old, university student, warm medium-brown skin, voluminous dark curly hair to the shoulders, natural makeup, white cotton t-shirt, light-wash jeans, small backpack. Friendly, expressive, Gen Z.
>
> PHONE: a slim smartphone in a deep wine/plum glossy finish, with a tall vertical pill-shaped camera module on the upper-left back holding two large round lenses and a separate ring flash below it.

## 0. Foto de referência (gerador de imagem: Gemini, Imagen, Midjourney ou similar)
> Photorealistic portrait photo, [CHARACTER], standing on a sunny university campus, soft natural light, looking at camera with a light smile, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16, ultra realistic skin texture, no text.

## 1. Selfie contra o sol (6 s), com fala
> Vertical 9:16 cinematic shot, photorealistic. [CHARACTER] on a university campus at golden hour, the sun directly behind her (strong backlight, lens flare). She raises [PHONE] to take a selfie, taps the shutter, then looks at the screen and frowns, mildly frustrated, and says in Brazilian Portuguese, natural and casual: "Quero tirar uma foto assim, mas nunca consigo deixar do jeito que eu quero!". Handheld feel, smooth, 35mm, shallow depth of field, realistic.

## 2. Mão tocando a tela (3 s): tela em verde para eu colocar a interface
> Vertical 9:16 macro close-up, photorealistic. A young woman's hand holds [PHONE] facing the camera; the phone screen is a flat pure chroma-key green (#00FF00), evenly lit, no reflections. Her thumb taps the lower-right area of the screen once. Soft bokeh campus background, golden hour backlight, cinematic.

## 3. Falando o pedido para o celular (4 s)
> Vertical 9:16 medium close-up, photorealistic. [CHARACTER] holds [PHONE] near her chin with the screen toward her (we see the phone's back), sun behind her, and speaks to the phone in Brazilian Portuguese, confident and casual: "Quero uma foto contra o sol, mas sem deixar meu rosto escuro." Natural lip sync, shallow depth of field, cinematic.

## 4. Foto "depois" (imagem): a selfie perfeita
> Photorealistic smartphone selfie, vertical 3:4, [CHARACTER] smiling, the sun directly behind her head creating a rim light on her hair, BUT her face is perfectly and naturally exposed, rich detail in the blue sky, campus buildings and trees visible behind her, HDR, natural colors, sharp, no text.

A versão escura ("antes") eu crio a partir dessa foto.

## 5. Reação (3 s)
> Vertical 9:16 close-up, photorealistic. [CHARACTER] looks at the screen of [PHONE], her eyes widen and she smiles with genuine excitement and says in Brazilian Portuguese: "Uau! Ficou perfeita!". Golden hour backlight, cinematic, shallow depth of field.

## 6. Pedido da segunda foto (3 s)
> Vertical 9:16 medium shot, photorealistic. [CHARACTER] on a running track holds [PHONE] and says playfully in Brazilian Portuguese: "Agora, uma foto minha correndo, sem ficar tremida!". Then she hands the phone off-screen. Sunny afternoon, cinematic.

## 7. Correndo (4 s)
> Vertical 9:16 tracking shot, photorealistic. [CHARACTER] runs toward the camera on a red running track in a sunny park, joyful, hair bouncing, energetic, slight motion blur on the background only, cinematic sports commercial look, 50mm.

## 8. Foto nítida correndo (imagem)
> Photorealistic sports photo, vertical 3:4, [CHARACTER] mid-stride running on a red running track, frozen motion, tack sharp, fast shutter 1/2000s, sunny, vibrant natural colors, no motion blur on her, no text.
